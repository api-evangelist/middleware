New Relic and Sentry both monitor production software but address different needs: New Relic provides full-stack observability covering applications, infrastructure, logs, and browsers, while Sentry is a developer-first tool focused on errors, traces, and session replay tied to specific commits. The article compares both tools across error tracking, APM, infrastructure monitoring, and pricing to help teams select the right observability solution.
