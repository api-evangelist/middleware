Middleware integrates with TrueFoundry AI Gateway so each inference request becomes a structured, queryable event correlated with infrastructure metrics, traces, and logs in a unified interface. This eliminates the need for customized instrumentation per model or provider, automatically capturing latency, token consumption, model performance, and completion status across AI vendors.
